§ 01 · Research

Toward guidance the clinic can trust.

i

My research develops self-contained mixed reality surgical navigation: augmented reality systems in which a single headset both senses the operative field and renders the patient's own anatomy onto it. By consolidating tracking, perception, and visualization into one device, I remove the external hardware that has long confined image guidance to specialized suites, moving it toward routine intraoperative use.

The systems I build pair deep learning perception, including fiducial detection, gaze-seeded SAM 2 segmentation, and voice control, with 3D surface reconstruction and nonrigid registration, so preoperative imaging deforms to match the living organ. Every stage is held to quantitative accuracy targets: hologram stability, depth sensing precision, and subsurface target error, validated from bench phantoms through in vivo porcine models.

i.

Self-Contained MR Navigation

A single Magic Leap 2 headset as both intraoperative sensor and AR display, eliminating external tracking hardware.

ii.

Deep-Learning Surgical Perception

Fiducial detection, gaze-seeded SAM 2 segmentation, and voice-driven interaction for a hands-free operative front end.

iii.

Intraoperative Surface Acquisition

Benchmarking time-of-flight sensing against learning-based multi-view RGB reconstruction, validated in vivo.

iv.

Deformable Registration

Non-rigid model-to-patient alignment for subsurface tumor targeting in liver and breast surgery.

§ 03 · Experience

Trajectory & training.

iii
2025 — Present

Postdoctoral Researcher

Vanderbilt University · Department of Biomedical Engineering · PI: Michael I. Miga
  • Leading development of a self-contained mixed reality navigation platform on the Magic Leap 2, where a single headset serves as both intraoperative sensor and AR display, removing external tracking hardware from the workflow.
  • Developed and benchmarked intraoperative surface-acquisition methods on the headset, comparing time-of-flight sensing with learning-based multi-view RGB reconstruction, with in vivo porcine demonstration.
